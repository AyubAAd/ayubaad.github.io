08 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Build, measure, iterate.

My work connects robot kinematics, embedded firmware, digital logic, instrumentation, and control. I like starting with a measurable requirement, building a model or prototype, comparing it with test observations, and documenting what still needs verification. I also enjoy explaining technical ideas through engineering peer mentoring.
